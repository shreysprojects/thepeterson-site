The important questions

Questions worth
an evening.

Each gathering begins with one question. Draw one from the deck, or bring your own.

  1. What would it take to build a company that is still worth working for in a hundred years?
  2. Which industry will be rebuilt from first principles before 2035, and who should be in the room when it is?
  3. Can financial success and lasting public good be measured on the same balance sheet?
  4. What do you believe about your field that most experts would still dispute?
  5. If you had to design one institution from scratch today, what would it be?
  6. Which bet looks reckless today and obvious in fifteen years?
  7. What does Montréal understand that London does not, and what has Shanghai seen that both have missed?
  8. How do you recognise good judgment before it has a track record?
  9. What should the next generation of founders refuse to optimise for?
  10. Which problem is too important to leave to a single discipline?
  11. What would you build with ten years, no pressure to exit, and the right three collaborators?
  12. Where will your industry’s best people come from in the next decade?

This adds it to the deck and opens the membership enquiry with your question filled in.
